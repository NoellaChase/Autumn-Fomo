🎙️ Summer FOMO — Day 1 Voice Memo

Recording Script for Noella Chase
Total Duration: ~2 minutes | Tone: Warm, personal, reassuring, slightly playful | Save As: day1-voice-memo.mp3
INTRO ~10 seconds

"Hey there... it's Noella.

I wanted to talk to you directly today. Not through text. Not through a fancy graphic. Just... me to you."

[PAUSE — 2 seconds]
THE PERMISSION ~45 seconds

"So here's the thing about summer FOMO...

Everyone's posting their perfect cottage weekends, their epic road trips, their 'living my best life' moments. And maybe you're sitting there thinking... 'I should be doing more.'

But I'm here to tell you something important:

You don't have to.

You really, truly, absolutely do NOT have to.

You have permission to do nothing today. To opt out. To say 'not for me, thanks.'

This isn't about being lazy. This isn't about missing out.

This is about choosing what actually fills YOUR cup."

[PAUSE — 3 seconds]
THE STORY ~35 seconds

"I created Summer FOMO because I was tired of feeling like I was failing at summer.

Like if I wasn't packing every weekend with activities, I was wasting the season.

But you know what? Some of my best summer days have been the quiet ones.

Reading on the porch.
Sipping coffee slowly.
Just... being.

No itinerary. No photos required. No performance."

[PAUSE — 2 seconds]
THE INVITATION ~20 seconds

"So today, I want you to do something radical.

Give yourself permission to do nothing.

Print out that certificate I made for you. Fill in your name. Put it somewhere you'll see it.

And when that little voice says 'you should be doing more'...

Point to the certificate and say: 'Nope. I'm officially doing nothing today.'"

[PAUSE — 2 seconds]
THE CLOSE ~10 seconds

"I'll be back tomorrow with something to help you actually say no to those summer plans you don't want to do.

Until then... enjoy the nothingness.

You've earned it.

Talk soon."

[END]

🎤 Recording Tips

  • Find a quiet space with minimal echo
  • Use your phone's Voice Memos app (iPhone) or Recorder (Android)
  • Speak like you're talking to a friend — natural is better than perfect
  • It's okay to stumble — just pause and re-record that section
  • Aim for 1:45–2:15 total length
  • Export/save as MP3: day1-voice-memo.mp3
