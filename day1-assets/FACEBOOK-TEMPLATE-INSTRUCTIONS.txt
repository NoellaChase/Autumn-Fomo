📘 How to Use Your Facebook Permission Slip

We've included a customizable permission slip so you can officially announce your "permission to do nothing" on Facebook!

Step 1: Download the PDF
Click the link above to download your permission slip template.

Step 2: Add Your Name
Open the PDF file on your computer or phone. You'll see a text field where it says "YOUR NAME HERE" — click/tap there and type your name.

Step 3: Save as Image
• iPhone: Take a screenshot of your completed permission slip, or use the "Share" button and save to Photos
• Android: Take a screenshot, or use a PDF-to-image converter app
• Computer: Use Snipping Tool (Windows) or Screenshot (Mac) to capture your completed slip

Step 4: Post to Facebook
Upload your permission slip image to Facebook with the hashtag #SummerFOMO — we can't wait to see your official "doing nothing" status! 🎉

Tip: You can post this to your feed, a story, or even as your cover photo if you're feeling bold!
