## Recording the Voice Memo:

1. Open Voice Memos (iPhone) or Recorder app (Android)
2. Read script in quiet room
3. Save and share to computer
4. Convert to MP3 if needed

**Option 2: Computer**
1. Use Audacity (free) or QuickTime
2. Record with headset or USB mic
3. Export as MP3

**Upload Options:**
- Your own server: `/audio/day1-voice-memo.mp3`

- Google Drive: Set to "Anyone with link can view"
