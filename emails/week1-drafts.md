## Day 6: The Introvert's Summer Survival Guide

**Subject:** For My Fellow Introverts: Your Summer Survival Guide

**Preview Text:** How to survive summer when everyone wants to "get together."

---

Hi [First Name],

Summer is designed for extroverts. Everyone wants to "get together," "hang out," and "make the most of the nice weather."

But what if the nice weather makes you want to stay inside with a book?

**Your Day 6 Download:**
The Introvert's Summer Survival Guide

**Energy Management Strategies:**
- Batch your social events (one per week max)
- Have an "out" planned before you arrive
- Drive yourself so you can leave when you want
- Host at your place (easier to control the exit)

**The "I'm Busy" Calendar:**
Block "me time" on your calendar like real appointments. When someone asks, "Are you free Tuesday?" you can honestly say, "I have plans."

**The Power of the Early Exit:**
"I can come for an hour!" is a complete sentence. You don't owe anyone your entire day.

**Your Day 6 Action:**
Block one evening this week as "introvert time." Protect it like a meeting with your boss.
